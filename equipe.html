01 / OUR ORIGINS

A DREAM THAT
FOUND ITS TRACK.

Vértice began in 2024 in a small workshop in São Paulo. A group of engineers, mechanics and drivers set out to turn their passion for motorsport into a team with an identity of its own.

From the first sketches to the first test, every step was a shared effort. Today, we bring that same spirit to the track: learn quickly, care about every detail and keep believing in the next breakthrough.
